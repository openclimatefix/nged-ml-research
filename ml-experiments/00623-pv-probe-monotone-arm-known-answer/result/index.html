    Monotone arm of the injected known-answer test of the donor-day PV probe

    A network forced to lower net demand as shortwave irradiance rises still recovers only about 70% of an injected real farm, against 68% for the plain network. Both networks recover a physics-curve injection almost exactly, so the probe works when the injected shape is the unit PV curve and loses about 30% when the injected shape is a real farm's. The real-farm bar (median recovery 0.80 to 1.20 at 4 of 6 Primaries) fails in both arms, at 0 of 6 Primaries each time. The stop rule (median recovery under 0.70 in both arms, or a placebo over 0.30) did not fire, because the monotone arm's median is 0.71 and the largest placebo read is 0.17.

    • Real-farm recovery is the same at both cutoffs (monotone 0.711 and 0.712; plain 0.682 and 0.683) and at both farm rotations, so the shortfall is not an accident of one cutoff or one farm-to-Primary assignment.
    • The interval fails because recovery is biased, not because it is narrow. The real-farm injections are covered 0% of the time in both arms, since the interval is centred on about 0.7 of the truth. The interval passes in the physics-curve world for the monotone arm (76%) and the plain arm (68%), and in the linear strong cloud-load world (75% and 69%).
    • The monotone arm does not lower the seed spread of the probed capacity. It is lower at D10, D14, and D9 and higher at D11, D16, and D5 (spread of the uninjected networks' probed capacity, the measure in the seed-spread figure; the spread of the injected differences is also lower at 3 of 6).
    • Validation loss. The mean validation negative log-likelihood of the uninjected networks is 0.467 (plain) and 0.477 (monotone) at 2026-03, and 0.472 and 0.484 at 2026-06. The CRPS on the same validation rows rises from 0.365 to 0.368 MW (2026-03) and 0.365 to 0.369 MW (2026-06) over all 16 Primaries (0.8% and 1.0%), and from 0.276 to 0.278 MW (2026-03) and 0.275 to 0.277 MW (2026-06) at the six MW Primaries. Each network's early stopping reads these rows, so the figures compare the arms and do not measure out-of-sample skill.

    The probed response against the unit PV curve

    The probed difference between an injected network and its twin falls linearly in the unit PV curve of the donor weather in both worlds and both arms. In the physics-curve world the lines follow the dashed 4 MW slope. In the real-farm world the slope is about 0.7 of it. The monotone constraint changes neither slope visibly. The cause of the flatter real-farm response is untested. A real farm's orientation, shading, and inverter clipping, which the unit curve lacks, are candidates.

    Placebos and the cloud-following load

    Both placebos read under 0.18 of the injected megawatts at every Primary in both arms. The monotone arm's shuffled-sky placebo reads slightly higher (0.13 to 0.17) than the plain arm's (0.09 to 0.16).

    Without the cloud template, both arms read a cloud-following load as PV (weak loads 0.11 to 0.23, strong loads 0.35 to 0.66 of a 2 MW farm). With the template, both pass. The plain network over-corrects (negative spurious capacity as large as -0.7 for the strong squared load), and the monotone network stays closer to zero (-0.2). Over-correction is not faking, but it shows that the template regression absorbs more than the cloud load alone in the plain network.

    Method

    • Monotone network (e00623_model.py). The weather encoder sees the other six weather columns with shortwave zeroed. A context network reads the datetime encoder's output and the Primary embedding and returns an amplitude, a slope, and an offset for each of 16 units. The shortwave effect h is the sum of softplus(amplitude) * softplus(softplus(slope) * sw + offset), which is non-decreasing in sw, and h is subtracted from every mixture component's mean. A sweep over 50 shortwave values on 2,000 random rows found no increase of the mixture means. Monotonicity holds with every other input fixed, so the other weather inputs (longwave, temperature, dew point) can still carry cloud information.
    • Everything else is experiment 621's (experiment 621): worlds, farms A, B, C, and F, rotations, cutoffs, seeds, probe, bootstrap, and the plain-arm results. The code in this folder is a copy of experiment 621's, with the model, the recovery bars, the arm comparison, and the validation CRPS script added. The pretraining takes about 26 s per network (about 25 to 29 s over the grid), so the 90 networks took about 0.7 GPU-hours.
    • Not run. The step world, as in experiment 621. The seed standard deviation of the probed capacity is 0.05 to 0.15 MW, so a 2 MW injection exceeds 4 seed standard deviations at every Primary, and the 2 MW injections were kept.
    • Earlier work. Experiment 129, 235, 269, 405, 474, 477, and 479 set the worlds, the placebos, and the recovery of 0.62 to 0.83 that this result matches.

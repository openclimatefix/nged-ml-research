    Can one neural network, trained to match the five-network pool, forecast Primary demand as well as the pool?

    Take-home message

    One network trained to copy the five-network pool scores like a single seed, not like the pool. At the eight MVA-only Primaries the single seed is 0.084 CRPS points worse than the pool, and at the eight every student sits within 0.006 points of the single seed. At the six the students are worse than the single seed by 0.012 (Dpool50) to 0.045 (Dpool80) points. The under-40% rule applies: under this recipe the pool's gain is not learnable by one network. The students match the tail coverage of a single teacher network.

    Question and answer

    Can one student network, fine-tuned to match the pool's quantile forecast, score within the minimum detectable effect of the pool? No. The pool's advantage over a single seed comes from averaging five independently fitted networks. A student that starts from the same pretrained weights and sees the teacher only on rows the teacher was fitted on scored no better than a single seed. The explanation is a likely cause, not a tested one.

    Checks and caveats

    • Recipe: monthly refits of experiment 425's folds (2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, 2026-06); the D15 and D18 start cut and the flagged exact-zero drop apply; the teacher is the same-month pool, so no scored actuals reach the student; the students start from the stored pretrained weights of the same seed, so student seed s shares its initialisation with teacher s. Code reviewed by one fresh Sonnet agent before the first fit (no bugs, three small fixes).
    • Design change from the brief: pinball loss against the teacher's quantile distribution, not against one value, because the point-target form made the timing fit's tails too wide (q05 coverage 0.027 against 0.049).
    • Early stopping sees the composite loss, so val_loss differs by alpha and is not compared across arms.
    • Not run: the mixture-teacher arm (dropped on the queue-order check), the earlier-months-only variant, EMA/SWA as a reference, example-forecast figures, and event plots. Every score is selection-biased; July 2026 is unused. NGED's event labels are incomplete, so "rows with no labelled event" may hold unlabelled events.
    • Christmas week and D15 and D18 are in work/scores/contrasts.csv (report-only). The pool's advantage over a single seed is 0.231 points at the eight and 0.141 at the six in Christmas week. It is 0.454 points at D15 and D18.
    • The cold-night minimum detectable effect is not estimated, so cold-night contrasts are descriptive. On rows with real events scored against normal-running-arrangement actuals (six_nra), the students score slightly better than the pool; the slice is in the contrasts file.

    What to try next

    • If the pool's gain is worth keeping, serve the pool; if one network is wanted, spend the effort on better single-network training, not on copying the pool.
    • Distillation could be retried with a teacher that is out of sample on the student's rows (a different month's pool on rows the teacher did not train on), at the cost of a leakage review.

    Plan (as registered)

    The plan as committed before any fit; the loss paragraph was updated once after the timing fit.

    Design

    • Teacher. The five same-month fine-tuned arm A networks of experiment 425 (work/models/A/<month>/s0..s4), all trained before the scored month. The teacher target of a row is the mean of the five networks' 11 sorted standardised quantiles, which is the quantile average that A-pool serves. Using another month's teacher would leak the scored actuals, so none is used.
    • Student. Experiment 425's pooled plain network, started from the stored pretrained arm A network of the same seed and fine-tuned on experiment 425's ENS fold rows at 2e-4 with experiment 397's loop (batch 16,384, at most 40 epochs, patience 6, gradient clip 1.0). The cuts of experiments 399 and 425 apply (the D15 and D18 start cut, the flagged exact-zero drop).
    • Loss. For a row, in standardised units: NLL(y) + alpha * P(q_s, teacher) + (1 - alpha) * P(q_s, y), multiplied by the fold's recency weight 0.5 ** (age_days / SHORT_HALF_LIFE_DAYS) and normalised by the batch's weight sum. NLL is the student's own mixture likelihood. P is the pinball loss summed over the 11 levels. Against the teacher it is the expected pinball loss over the teacher's quantiles as atoms (the scorers' level masses), because a pinball loss against one teacher value is asymmetric and widened the student's tail quantiles in the timing fit (q05 coverage 0.027 against the teacher's 0.049). Student quantiles are differentiable through the implicit function theorem on the mixture CDF. Early stopping sees the same composite loss on the held-out weeks, unweighted.
    • Arms (3 seeds, 5 folds of experiment 449: 2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, 2026-06):
      • Dpool50: alpha 0.5;
      • Dpool80: alpha 0.8;
      • DpoolERA5: alpha 0.5, plus one batch per step (16,384 rows) of the ERA5-and-CAMS rows that pretrained the teacher, with the teacher term only and equal row weights. The teacher outputs on those rows come from the same five networks.
      • References: A-pool (the teacher, stored), and S0 (single seeds 0 to 2 of arm A, stored).
    • Not run. D-mix (distilling the mixture of arm A seeds and lagless networks): dropped on the manager's queue-order check. The variant that distils only from rows of months before the student's validation month: not run. EMA/SWA is read from the weight-averaging experiment's page when it merges.
    • Teacher in sample. Every teacher is in sample on the fine-tuning rows, so its quantiles are sharper there than on new months. The 0.5 true-target mix and the student's own likelihood limit the copying. A student cannot exceed an honest teacher. The log under work/logs/ stores coverage at q05, q95, and q99 of the teacher and each student on the held-out weeks and on training rows.
    • Fits. 45 student fits (3 arms x 5 folds x 3 seeds), plus five teacher passes per fold (inference). One fit takes about 128 s on the GPU (experiment 425's fit, for comparison, takes 30 to 37 s with the forecast). Estimated 2.3 GPU-hours in total.
    • Monthly refits. The stored folds are the monthly refits of experiment 425. The students are scored at the same cadence.

    Scoring

    Rows are experiment 470's intersection (experiment_470_intersection_v1). Forecasts are widened by 1.15 as in experiment 470. Slices: the eight, the signed-MW six on rows with no labelled event, cold winter nights, and the 12 to 36 hour band; Christmas week and D15 and D18 are report-only. The month-block bootstrap with Bonferroni 27 intervals, absolute CRPS beside every contrast, July 2026 unused, every score selection-biased.

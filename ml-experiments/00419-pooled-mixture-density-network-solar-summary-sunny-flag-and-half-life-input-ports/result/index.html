    Do the solar summary, the sunny flag, and a different row-weight half-life, which helped the XGBoost model, also help the pooled mixture density network?

    Take-home message: the XGBoost model's solar-summary input did not carry over to the pooled plain neural network, and neither did the shorter half-life, so none of the input changes is adopted.

    The solar summary cannot be told apart from seed noise. Adding the daily mean of the ensemble's shortwave radiation (sol) lowered the continuous ranked probability score (CRPS) at the six signed-MW Primaries (primary substations whose power flows both ways, measured in signed megawatts) by only 0.012 points of capacity (-0.043 to +0.013). The change is one sixth of the minimum detectable effect (0.0738 points, twice the fold-to-fold spread of the difference between two sets of random seeds).

    A refit with new seeds moved the MVA-only eight more than the solar summary did. The same neural network refitted with new seeds (ctrl_s3) moved the MVA-only eight (the Primaries measured in megavolt-amperes) by -0.047 (-0.075 to -0.022), about six times as far as sol did there (-0.008). At the signed-MW six the refit moved the CRPS by +0.011, the same size as sol.

    The sunny flag with a 1-year half-life, and the 0.25-year half-life, did not help. Adding the sunny flag and a 1-year half-life (bundle) was worse than sol by +0.033 and +0.037. The 0.25-year half-life (hl91r, sol_hl91) raised the signed-MW six CRPS by +0.044 (+0.004 to +0.099) and +0.048 (+0.006 to +0.088) while lowering the D15 and D18 CRPS, as experiment 407's 0.25-year half-life did.

    The reference ctrl_w115 stays. Whether a solar summary helps this neural network needs more than three seeds or more than five test months. The verdict is not supported. Every number comes from five test months, so all are interim.

    Predictions

    • S1 failed. sol lowers the signed-MW six CRPS by 0.012 (-0.043 to +0.013). The prediction asked for at least the minimum detectable effect (0.0738) and at least 0.02; the interval spans zero. At the MVA-only eight the change is -0.008 (-0.045 to +0.021).
    • S2 failed. bundle is +0.033 above sol at the signed-MW six and +0.037 at the MVA-only eight, outside the 0.03 limit at both.
    • S3 held. hl91r against ctrl_s3 lowers the D15 and D18 CRPS by 0.264 (-0.904 to +0.106), the same sign as experiment 407 (-0.183). It lowers the cold-night CRPS at the signed-MW six by 0.051 (-0.153 to +0.038), the same sign as experiment 407's signed-MW six cold-night value (-0.162), and at D15 and D18 by 0.116 (-1.835 to +0.233). On cold nights at the MVA-only eight the change is +0.023 (-0.049 to +0.083). Experiment 407's -0.162 is a signed-MW six value, so the MVA-only eight figure is not an exception to the prediction as made. The plan does not name the group for the cold-night half of S3, so this page reports all three. The rise at the signed-MW six is +0.044, inside the 0.10 limit.
    • S4 held. sol_hl91 against sol lowers the D15 and D18 CRPS by 0.407 (-1.257 to +0.138), the same sign as S3.
    • The sunny April-to-June prediction failed and decides nothing. sol is +0.010 against the reference at the signed-MW six, not at least 0.05 lower.

    Decision

    The hypothesis is not supported, by the decision rule as written. S1 fails, so the hypothesis is not supported on the solar-summary half. For the partly supported route, hl91r would need to lower the signed-MW six or MVA-only eight CRPS against ctrl_s3 as a counted gain; it raised both (+0.044 and +0.043), so that route is closed. No arm is carried forward: sol and bundle failed their predictions, and the intervals of hl91r and sol_hl91 at the signed-MW six lie wholly above zero. D15 and D18 and the cold-night slice are report-only and did not enter the decision.

    Components adopted or not adopted

    • Adopted, unchanged. The reference ctrl_w115: the pooled plain neural network widened by 1.15, with a 0.5-year row-weight half-life, three Gaussian components, and the D15 and D18 start cut.
    • Not adopted: sol. The two daily-mean shortwave columns lowered the CRPS by 0.012 at the signed-MW six, one sixth of the minimum detectable effect.
    • Not adopted: bundle. The sunny flag and the 1-year half-life made sol worse.
    • Not adopted: hl91r and sol_hl91. The 0.25-year half-life raised the signed-MW six CRPS with intervals wholly above zero. The lower D15 and D18 CRPS, lower q99 pinball loss, and lower curtailment cost of the two 0.25-year arms are report-only and carry wide intervals.
    • Not fitted: temp. The trailing temperature means were left to a separate experiment.

    Limits

    • Selection twice. Experiments 381 and 407 both picked their arms on months that include these five, so a gain here would have been selected twice. No arm gained, so the limit only adds to the caution on hl91r and sol_hl91.
    • Five interim months. The month-block bootstrap has wide intervals, most of all at D15 and D18, and no claim of benefit rests on any number here.
    • The partial first and last local day. The daily mean in the first local day of every numerical weather prediction (NWP) run covers only the afternoon and evening. The last local day holds one or two near-midnight steps. The neural network therefore sees a different quantity at days 1 and 2 than later. The solar summary of experiment 381 was built the same way, from the control member instead of the ensemble mean.
    • The month-block bootstrap understates seed noise. In this experiment one refit with new seeds moved the MVA-only eight CRPS by -0.047 (-0.075 to -0.022), which suggests the month-block bootstrap understates the noise. Telling an input change from seed noise at that group needs more than three seeds or more than five test months.
    • The minimum detectable effect is noisy. It rests on five fold differences from one pair of seed sets, and the MVA-only eight moved by 0.047 between the same two sets.
    • ctrl_s3 differs from the reference. The refit with seeds 3 to 5 scores 3.288 against 3.277 at the signed-MW six and 3.811 against 3.858 at the MVA-only eight, so the two seed sets are not interchangeable.
    • hl91r differs from experiment 407's hl91. hl91r uses seeds 3 to 5 and a half-life of exactly 0.25 years (91.3125 days), against seeds 0 to 2 and 91 days: hl91_w115 scored 3.350, 3.883, and 8.471 in experiment 407 (signed-MW six, MVA-only eight, D15 and D18) against 3.333, 3.854, and 8.349 for hl91r.
    • Unlogged load-shift events. National Grid Electricity Distribution (NGED) says load-shift events may be missing from its logs, so the training rows and the scoring rows can hold events no label covers.
    • Cleaned data. Training rows drop every half-hour that the cleaned power table flags as an exact zero, and every row whose lag reaches one. The scorer dropped 1,179 flagged half-hours from the actuals of every arm, including the stored reference. Training rows dropped per fold: 2,155 (2025-10), 2,846 (2025-12), 2,886 (2026-01), 2,899 (2026-03), and 2,925 (2026-06). Every arm's scored keys match the reference's, with 348,192 rows in each of the first four folds and 336,960 in 2026-06.
    • The curtailment cost has no interval. The scorer prints one total per arm.

    Plan as committed before any fit

    Question and design

    We add one XGBoost input change at a time to the pooled plain neural network and compare each change with the same neural network widened by 1.15. Experiment 381 found that two columns holding the daily mean of the ensemble's shortwave radiation, today and yesterday, lowered the XGBoost model's CRPS by 0.051 points at the signed-MW six. The pooled plain neural network of experiment 397 has the solar geometry (cosine of the zenith, sine and cosine of the azimuth) and the ensemble mean and spread of shortwave at the valid time, but not that daily summary and not a cloud-aware sunny flag.

    • The neural networks. The pooled plain neural network of experiment 397 (setting g09, summary scheme, an 8-wide Primary embedding, three Gaussian components) on its five screen folds (test months 2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, and 2026-06), refitted monthly, three seeds averaged in quantile space, with the same scoring rows. Every arm is widened by 1.15 about the median on q01, q05, q95, and q99, as the reference is.
    • ref and ctrl_s3. The reference ctrl_w115 stored by experiment 407 (seeds 0 to 2; not refitted), and the same neural network fitted again with seeds 3 to 5, which gives the minimum detectable effect and a seed-matched reference for hl91r.
    • sol. Adds shortwave_day_mean and shortwave_prev_day_mean (experiment 381's arm A2), computed from the ensemble-mean shortwave of the same NWP run over the local day of the valid time and over the previous local day, standardised on each fold's training rows like the other weather inputs. A previous-day mean missing at the start of a run is replaced by the day's own mean, and a daily mean cut short by the end of the 14-day horizon uses the steps available.
    • hl91r. The reference with row weights that halve every 0.25 years, with seeds 3 to 5. The 0.25-year half-life is carried from experiment 407, whose hl91 used seeds 0 to 2.
    • sol_hl91. sol with the 0.25-year half-life carried from experiment 407.
    • bundle. sol plus the sunny flag and sunny_times_shortwave (experiment 381's arm A4: the flag is 1 where the ensemble-mean shortwave exceeds 0.6 times the Haurwitz clear-sky irradiance) with row weights that halve every 1 year: experiment 381's weekly bundle, refitted monthly.
    • temp. Adds trailing temperature means and heating degrees (experiment 396's arm W1). It is not fitted here, and a separate experiment tests it if experiment 396 reports that W1 beats its noise floor.
    • The start cut. D15 and D18 lose every training row before 2025-03-01, and every row whose weekly lag reaches back before that date (as in experiments 406 and 407). The scoring rows start in 2025-10, so no scoring actual or inference input is affected.
    • Not in this experiment. The sunny flag alone and a 1-year half-life alone, because experiment 381 (-0.002) and experiment 407's hl365 (+0.009) already tested them.

    Components adopted from earlier results

    • Adopted. One pooled neural network with a Primary embedding (experiment 403); the ensemble mean and spread passed through the neural network once (experiments 397 and 394); monthly refits; the 1.15 widening and the 0.5-year half-life of the control (experiment 407: no tail arm and no other half-life beat the widened control at the signed-MW six); and the D15 and D18 start cut (experiment 399).
    • Carried for retest. The 0.25-year half-life, because it lowered the score at D15 and D18 and on cold nights in experiment 407 although its interval at the signed-MW six included zero.
    • Not adopted. Attention over input groups (experiment 397: the gate stayed within 0.01 of uniform), the differentiable physics neural network (experiment 413: worse than the plain neural network by 0.17 and 0.23 points), and any tail arm (experiment 407).

    Standing practices followed, and the deviations

    • Followed. Monthly refits; every fitted neural network saved in the private data folder; stored forecasts reused as references and never refitted; a few-fold screen with every number labelled interim; tails (coverage and pinball loss at q01, q05, q95, and q99, on all half-hours and on sunny low-demand half-hours) and the avoidable-curtailment cost reported beside the CRPS; example forecast charts; a fresh Sonnet plan review and a fresh Sonnet code review before the first fit.
    • Cleaned data. Training rows drop every half-hour that the cleaned power table flags as an exact zero (substation_zero) and every row whose lag reaches one, and the scorer drops flagged half-hours from the actuals of every arm including the references. The number of rows dropped is printed for each fold. The reference was fitted with the same rule in experiment 407.
    • Departure: the sanity contrast. Experiment 407's ctrl differs from experiment 406's pooled plain neural network by -0.021 points at the signed-MW six, which is the size of the change that the cleaned training rows made. Differences below that size are not read as effects.

    Fit counts, time, and memory

    • Fits. sol, hl91r, sol_hl91, bundle, and ctrl_s3: five arms by five folds by three seeds, so 75 fits. The widening is the fixed factor 1.15, not a fitted factor.
    • Time. About 40 to 50 seconds per fit on the GPU, so about 1 hour in total.
    • Run recipe. One GPU process under systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=8G -p OOMPolicy=continue with one feeder core. The scorer runs in passes of a few arms, because holding every arm's rows at once needs more than 14 GB.
    • Stored. Forecasts per arm, fold, and seed (time_series_id, power_fcst_init_time, valid_time, lead_hours, q01 to q99) and every fitted neural network, in this experiment's private folder. The scorer matches the scored keys and the row count of every arm to the reference and prints the row counts per fold. The screen months are the five of experiment 407, so its stored reference is reused.

    Checks the write-up reports

    • Absolute CRPS of every arm beside the reference and experiment 406's per-Primary neural network, per group and per Primary.
    • Coverage and pinball loss at the four outer levels, on all half-hours and on sunny low-demand half-hours (the sunny half-hours whose reference median is in the lowest quarter for the Primary).
    • The CRPS on sunny April-to-June half-hours, on cold nights, and on Economy 7 step hours.
    • The cost of avoidable curtailment at the scaled export limit (studies.node_curtailment, 0.9 of the limit, swept to 0.75 and 0.5), if the scorer offers it.
    • The CRPS on sunny April-to-June half-hours and on sunny low-demand half-hours, both defined from experiment 381's flag and the reference's median for every arm, as in experiment 407.
    • The tails (coverage at q95 and q99) as a separate screen beside the CRPS.
    • Audit checks: the difference between the median's error and the mean's error beside each contrast, and the results re-derived by a fresh Sonnet agent before the write-up.
    • Example forecasts for D10 (signed-MW) and D4 (MVA-only): a day-ahead forecast and a full 14-day forecast from one run, in winter (made in January) and summer (made in June), of the best arm.

    Caveats registered before the scores

    • The solar summary is built from the ensemble mean. Experiment 381 built it from the control member, so a difference from that experiment's effect may come from the mean.
    • Experiment 381 chose its arms on the year it scored, so its -0.051 is an upper bound.

    • The plan was reviewed against the evidence since the programme brief: experiment 407's hl365 and experiment 381 already tested the 1-year half-life and the sunny flag alone, 413's physics neural network did not advance, and 396's arm W1 has not reported, so temp is left out.

    • The first and last local day of every run are partial. Every NWP run starts at 09:30 UTC and lasts 14 days, so the first local day's daily mean covers only the afternoon and evening, and the last local day holds one or two near-midnight steps. Training and inference build the columns the same way, and experiment 381 did too, so the columns are consistent; the neural network sees a different quantity at days 1 and 2 than later.

    • The half-lives are exactly 0.25 and 1 year (91.3125 and 365.25 days), against 91 and 365 days in experiment 407, so the comparison with 407's hl91 is close but not exact.
    • The minimum detectable effect is noisy, because it rests on five fold differences from one pair of seed sets.

    Counting towards the demand-v2 stop rule

    This experiment does not count. It tests input changes on an existing model, not a new demand model, so no idea's count rises whatever the verdict.

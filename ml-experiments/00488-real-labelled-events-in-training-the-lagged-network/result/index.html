    Do real labelled events, mixed into training or used to fine-tune, improve the lagged network?

    Real labelled load-shift events (periods when NGED moved a substation's load to a neighbouring substation), added to the training of the lagged-power neural network, change nothing detectable. The question was whether the real events from months before the scored month improve the lagged-power neural network (A0-inj of experiment 404, a mixture density network that reads the three most recent power lags and was trained on synthetic events injected into the data) when mixed into training or used to fine-tune it. The answer at the signed-MW six is no: RealMix (real events mixed into training) is within the minimum detectable effect (MDE) of A0-inj on every row class, and PT-FT (pretrained on injected events, then fine-tuned on real ones) is worse on four of the five row classes. The result narrows the search: the label-free recipes that could replace real labels (injected or scanned events) have nothing to match beyond the target correction.

    Figures

    Arms, rows, and method

    • Terms. The signed-MW six are the six Primary substations that report signed power (D5, D9, D10, D11, D14, D16). A harm row is one whose target lies outside every event but whose lags lie inside one. The 1.15 widening scales each quantile's distance from the median by 1.15. The lagless table holds forecasts made without power lags.

    • Network and recipe. Experiment 404's A0-inj (PlainLagMdn, injected world of seed 100, 6 epochs, experiment 397's recency weights), monthly refit, seed 0, five scored months (2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, 2026-06). One fit takes about 78 s on the GPU; the 18 fits took about 0.4 GPU-hours. Code in ml-experiments/00488-*, importing experiments 404, 428, 435, 400, and 397 without edits.

    • Arms. RealMix: rows whose target lies inside an event that ended at least 14 days before the scored month get the corrected target (actual minus experiment 435's NRA share, depth 1.0) and the observed lags; the uncorrected copies are removed. PT-FT: stored A0-inj, 2 epochs at one tenth of the learning rate, on the corrected event rows plus an equal number of rows with no labelled event from the same months. RealMix-shuf: each event's lag pattern and depth injected into a random clean period of the same Primary, with the clean target. repro: the A0-inj recipe through this experiment's loop. RealMix-d075 and RealMix-d125: RealMix with the correction scaled, 2026-06 only. +LL marks the 50/50 mixture with the cross-fitted lagless table, as B-mix does for A0-inj.
    • Training events. At the signed-MW six, 20, 28, 29, 38, and 43 events were available to the five months (64 to 113 at all 16 Primaries); no month was dropped. Rows whose target lies inside an available event are 3.45% of 2026-06 training rows, above the 2% target, so RealMix does not oversample and differs from A0-inj only by the corrected target and the observed lags on those rows.
    • Scored rows (signed-MW six, seed 0, five months pooled): 604,246 injected harm rows, 533,170 injected rows with no event, 323,235 rows with no labelled event, 44,145 real harm rows, 50,168 rows inside a padded real event (24 events), and 12,654 rows in the 96 half-hours after one. The three scoring worlds are 200 to 202; the real world is -1. Flagged zeros are dropped as in experiment 404; they were not tallied again here.
    • MDE. Seed spread of A0-inj at 2026-06 (seeds 0 to 2), for a five-month pool: 0.09 on injected harm rows, 0.13 inside events, 0.08 on rows with no labelled event, and 0.31 on real harm rows. The month-to-month spread of each contrast's own monthly differences gives larger values (0.26 on injected harm rows, 0.74 on corrected event rows, 0.25 on rows with no labelled event). The seed-based values (0.08 to 0.13) overlap P1's predicted 0.05 to 0.2, and the month-to-month values (0.25 to 0.74) exceed it, so P1 could mostly be reported as "within the MDE".

    Serving requirements and the accuracy lost by simplifying

    • RealMix and PT-FT serve one network per pass, with no lagless table, no mixture step, and no detector. Their accuracy loss against B-mix, which serves two networks and a refreshed table, is +0.29 and +1.49 on injected harm rows and +0.25 and +1.33 on rows with no labelled event.
    • RealMix+LL serves the same two networks and table as B-mix and matches it within the MDE.

    Checks

    • Median minus mean forecast, and the error of the mean forecast: not computed. The stored forecasts hold the 11 quantiles only, so the mean forecast is unavailable.
    • Audit triggers: none fired. The control that should change nothing (repro) moves the score by 0.000, no nested arm beats the arm that contains it, and the intervals are similar in width to those of experiment 404 and 455. A fresh Sonnet agent re-derived the injected-harm and no-labelled-event contrasts of RealMix and PT-FT from the stored forecasts with its own code, and matched them to three decimals.
    • Prediction outcome: P1 reported as within the MDE for RealMix; PT-FT is worse than A0-inj, not better. P2 held (RealMix-shuf does not improve on A0-inj). P3 held (no arm beats B-mix on rows with no labelled event). P4 held (3.45% natural share).

    Caveats and next steps

    • NGED's event labels are incomplete. Rows with no labelled event may hold unlabelled events, so a real-event slice undercounts and the clean-row figures are optimistic.
    • This is a diagnostic. At about 1,300 substations NGED will supply no event labels, so a gain here could not ship without a label-free way to find events; none was found.
    • Not run: avoidable curtailment cost. The experiment 449 pipeline depends on experiment 448's stored rows and reference forecasts, which this experiment's rows do not match. Every contrast above is within the MDE or worse than A0-inj, so a curtailment difference would be small; the scoring can be added if wanted.
    • Not run: seeds 1 and 2 of the new arms. The MDE uses A0-inj's and B-mix's stored seeds; each new arm has seed 0 only. The sweep ran for 2026-06 only.
    • Depth error. The corrected target uses the labelled depth; the 0.75 and 1.25 arms ran for one month.
    • Next. A label-free recipe has only the target correction to replace; whether a scanned or injected stand-in matches RealMix is not worth testing, because RealMix itself gains nothing.

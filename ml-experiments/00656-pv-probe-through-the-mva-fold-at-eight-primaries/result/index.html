    Can a solar-capacity probe still work when the substation meter reports apparent power?

    The run is inconclusive: the pre-registered gate failed after one cutoff, but the failure came from a flaw in bar A1 and from the starting capacities of the fold fit. The linear probe from experiment 621 loses most of its signal once an added solar farm can push a substation's flow through zero. A probe that models the fold recovered 0.76 to 0.82 of an added fleet's megawatts at 0.5 to 2 times the midday reading, and 0.88 at 6 times only after the starting capacities were widened following the failure (post hoc).

    We asked whether the unmetered solar capacity behind a substation can be read when the meter reports apparent power (megavolt-amperes, which never goes negative) rather than signed megawatts. Six Primary substations report signed megawatts. We converted their flow to synthetic apparent power, added a solar fleet of known size to the data, and trained a neural network on the result. The probe then holds the calendar inputs of that neural network fixed, feeds it the weather of other days, and regresses its output on a solar-output curve. The slope is the capacity it reads, in megawatts of alternating current (AC). We compare the capacity read from a neural network trained with the fleet against its twin trained without the fleet. Only the 2026-06 cutoff ran: 24 conditions with 3 seeds each is 72 neural networks, at 23 seconds per fit on the graphics processing unit (GPU).

    Terms used below.

    • MW Primaries and MVA Primaries. The six MW Primaries (D5, D9, D10, D11, D14, D16) are metered in signed megawatts. The eight MVA Primaries are metered in apparent power: D2, D3, D4, D19, and D21 never come near zero flow on a sunny midday, and D8, D12, and D22 do (they "fold").
    • The fold. Apparent power is the length of a real-power part and a reactive-power part. When solar output exceeds demand, the reading falls to the reactive floor and then rises again, so a linear fit reads the wrong sign.
    • Midday reading. A Primary's median flow over sunny half-hours from 10:00 to 15:00 UTC in April to August, in MW.
    • Injection level. The AC capacity of the added fleet as a multiple of the midday reading (0.5×, 1×, 2×, 6×). A fleet is half a real metered farm's output shape and half a physics mixture.
    • Recovery. The capacity read from the neural network trained with the fleet, minus the capacity read from its twin, divided by the fleet's AC capacity. The values below are medians over seeds and Primaries.
    • Probes. L0 is the linear probe of experiment 621. L1 is L0 on rows well above the reactive floor. F4 is the fold model with four solar-output curves, F1 with one, and FA is F4 with a prior on each probe cell's demand from the night reading.
    • Worlds. In the power-factor (PF) world the reactive power is 0.25 times the median real power of that half-hour of the week (power factor 0.97). In the floor world the reactive power is a constant. In the native world the five non-folding MVA Primaries take the same transformation.
    • Tier A and Tier B. Tier A gates a capacity table for the five MVA Primaries that do not fold. Tier B asks whether capacity can be read through the fold, where D8, D12, and D22 stay report-only.

    Post hoc: widening the fold fit's starting capacities fixes the failure at 6×

    The audit found that the fold fit sits on its zero-capacity bound at almost every MW Primary in the 6× worlds. The fit started at 0.25, 1, and 3 times the midday reading, and a true capacity of 6 times lies beyond every start. Starting also at 6 and 8 times, post hoc and with no retraining, gives:

    Condition (median over seeds and the six MW Primaries) FA F4 F1
    Fleet 0.5× 0.74 0.67 0.70
    Fleet 1× 0.70 0.64 0.72
    Fleet 2× 0.80 0.78 0.71
    Fleet 6× 0.88 0.83 0.72
    Physics mixture 6× 0.94 0.87 0.78
    Sun-rising load (placebo; no solar added) -0.11 -0.13 -0.09

    FA at 6× reads 0.99 at D11 and 0.63 at D5, and the five native MVA Primaries read 0.91. No bar is re-scored with these rows.

    Figure: the cloud-following load is the placebo that fails. The fold model reads it at 0.62 of its size and the linear probe at 0.44, so neither probe separates solar output from demand that follows the clouds. Dots at the same level as the reference row would count as a read-out of real solar.

    Deviations from the plan

    • Fold fit. Three starting capacities (0.25, 1, and 3 times the midday reading) in place of a profile over 121 capacities; 40 bootstrap draws in place of 200; 40 donor rows per probe cell. The post hoc starts at 6 and 8 times are labelled above.
    • A1. Compared with the signed world at 1×, as above. Stop rule 3 (stop if A1 fails) fired as pre-registered.
    • Fleet. The physics half of the fleet is driven by the Primary's own sky, with no offset to a neighbouring pixel. The boxcar placebo is capped at the Primary's midday reading per event, because an event sized to the daily energy of the 2× fleet exceeded the load.
    • L1 probe. The floor estimate in the one-sided probe's threshold is capped at half the midday reading.
    • Native worlds apply only in the PF world.
    • Not run. The 2026-03 cutoff, the signed-world energy conversion, and the example S(u) curves beyond their class (falling at 13 Primaries, rising at D12).

    Caveats

    • The reactive power of the real meters is unknown, and power factor 0.97 is an assumption.
    • NGED's event labels are incomplete, so the clean rows may hold unlabelled events.
    • D8's flow regime changed in 2025, and the neural network has no year input, so it averages over the change.
    • Nothing here is replicated: one cutoff and one set of farms.

    Checks

    • Forecast checks: the experiment scores no forecast, so the median-minus-mean and quantile-score checks do not apply.
    • Audit triggers that fired: a control failed (A1), the result contradicted its prediction for Tier B at 6× (explained by the audit), and a follow-up would depend on the unreplicated 0.79 at 2×. A leave-one-site-out flip and a nested-arm win did not fire. The audit report is e656_audit.md in the private briefs folder.
    • Audit triage: accepted finding 1 (the post hoc table), accepted finding 3 (the A1 caveat), and accepted the trigger list (finding 5). Finding 2 reproduced the headline numbers, and finding 4 found no bug in the world construction.
    • Code review (Sonnet): accepted the boxcar cap, the sun-rising-load scaling, the arm-specific night reading, the basis-curve normalisation, and native worlds in the PF world only. Rejected the profile-likelihood deviation as a defect, and listed it above.
    • Write-up review: accepted the A2 and rounding corrections, the missing captions, and the figure fixes.

    What the next experiment should answer

    Does the fold model with a night prior keep its 0.7 to 0.9 recovery at a second cutoff and a second set of farms, with a signed twin at every level, starting capacities out to 8 times, and an injection that is not sized by D5's small midday reading? Does it still pass the sun-rising-load placebo, and can anything separate solar output from the cloud-following load that it misreads?

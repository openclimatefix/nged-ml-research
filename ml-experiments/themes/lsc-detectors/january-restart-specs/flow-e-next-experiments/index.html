    Spec: FLOW-E next experiments

    This is a copy of the answer that an Opus sub-agent gave the Discuss session on 2026-11-09, trimmed of conversation. Every number in it is the sub-agent's own analysis of the stored outputs of experiment 651 and is unaudited. Scores are CRPS in points of capacity, on event-free rows at 24 to 120 hours, in the development months 2025-10 and 2025-12 only. No row on or after 2026-07-01 was read. None of the three proposals has run.

    Take-home

    • FLOW-E's only measured cost comes almost entirely from one probable unlabelled event. I recomputed FLOW-E minus G0 at the six MW Primaries as +0.022 (the page gives +0.021). Five Primary-days at D10, 8 to 12 December 2025, carry 62% of that excess. Dropping D10's rows for 1 to 16 December 2025 (5% of the six MW rows) leaves +0.003, and the two seeds then disagree in sign, as they do for every other arm.
    • In those days D10 runs 15 to 21 points of capacity above G0's median for about ten days, and the forecasts of its electrical neighbours are off too. D12 runs 25 to 34 points above from 8 December, D15 13 to 60 points above, and D11 about 5 points below from 3 to 7 December. NGED's labels mark every one of those D10 rows event-free. FLOW-E lowers D10's median by about 0.7 points there, which is what a normal-running-arrangement (NRA) forecast should do if this was a transfer. I cannot verify that it was a transfer.
    • The same episode also inflates the class 2 recipe's "excess over A1". G0 minus A1 at the six MW Primaries is +0.141 on all rows and +0.078 without D10's rows for 1 to 16 December. D10 itself goes from +0.45 to −0.16. A1 follows load shifts most (0.119 on bank M), so A1 scores best on an unlabelled shift. The remaining excess sits mostly at D16 (+0.29 in October, +0.32 in December).
    • FLOW-E's follow gain needs a metered mirror. On bank X (no mirror) FLOW-E follows 0.059, exactly G0's figure. Its whole gain on banks M and S (0.038 against G0's 0.059 and C's 0.044 and 0.046) comes from reading the neighbour that received the load.
    • So FLOW-E has not yet been shown to cost anything on ordinary days, and it holds follow. The cheapest informative step is the confirmation run that experiment 651's gate cancelled. That run needs a rewired-graph placebo and the probable unlabelled event handled the same way for every arm.

    Brief facts checked

    Claim in the brief Check
    Antisymmetric MW flow on each of 29 electrical edges; per-node correction = minus net outflow over capacity, times a learnt scale starting at zero Correct (e00651_nbmodel.flow_correction). The flow neural network reads only each end's two current gap values (day lag and week lag), plus the log capacity ratio, and no gap history as the design proposed. The correction is hidden on 50% of training rows.
    Auxiliary loss on the known synthetic flow, weight 0.1 Not quite. The loss is on the per-node lag correction, not the edge flow. It is (correction + injected lag shift)² / 0.2², and it applies on every training row with a present lag: target zero on unshifted rows, on all rows whether or not messages are hidden.
    Two months, two seeds, fine-tuning only; pretraining from experiment 591's chain Correct. The graph layer exists only in step 3.
    Follow on bank M 0.038 against 0.044; six MW CRPS 2.479 against 2.458 (+0.021, interval +0.010 to +0.032); q99 pinball 0.196 against 0.188 Correct (day-block 95% interval). The FLOW-E minus C leave-one-Primary-out contrast never flips: +0.017 to +0.028.
    The six MW excess over A1 (0.13) is the same for G0 Correct as stated, but about 45% of the excess is the D10 episode above.
    Seed noise exceeds every contrast except FLOW-E's Correct on all rows. Without the D10 episode it exceeds FLOW-E's as well.

    Training data of experiment 651 (from e00418_prep, e00448_run.pretrain_rows, e00574_fit, e00397_prep, and the logs of experiment 591):

    • Step 1, pretraining: estimates of past weather (ERA5 means and CAMS shortwave) in pseudo-runs every 2 days from 2019-09-01, at leads of 0.5 to 351 h. Five Primaries start later after early-calibration cuts (D11 2019-09-17, D10 2019-12-01, D5 2020-02-01, D14 2020-07-01, D16 2021-02-01). Each fold has 7.0 to 7.9 million rows, ending at the start of the fold's month minus the cutoff age.
    • Step 2: a fresh mixer fitted on the ECMWF ENS history from 2024-04-01 to the cutoff, with a 3-day embargo.
    • Step 3, the step in which FLOW-E was trained: a daily fine-tune refitted on days 0 and 14 of each fold, on ENS rows with E3 rows and experiment 581's lag-only synthetic shifts.
    • Not used: the 2021-03 to 2024-03 ENS backfill from Source Cooperative.

    Ranked proposals

    1. FLOW-C: confirm FLOW-E on all five development months with a rewired placebo (run first)

    • Question. Does FLOW-E keep a lower follow than G0 and the Candidate (C) across five months, and does the real electricity-network wiring beat a rewired graph?
    • Architecture changes. None to FLOW-E. Add FLOW-R, which is FLOW-E on a degree-preserving rewired graph, using experiment 651's GAT-R rewiring. Store the per-node correction (flow_scale × share) per row, so that it can be scored as a detector.
    • Training data. The same as experiment 651, so that G0 and C stay matched. The full 2019-onward ERA5 and CAMS history is already in step 1. This experiment adds none of it to the flow layer, because that would confound the confirmation: proposal 2 tests the full history.
    • Arms. G0, FLOW-E, and FLOW-R, fitted on 2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, and 2026-06 with seeds 0 to 2. The stored G0 and FLOW-E fits for 2025-10 and 2025-12 at seeds 0 and 1 are reused. C comes from the stored seed 0 to 2 forecasts of experiments 612 and 613. Banks M, S, and X.
    • Unlabelled-event screen, fixed before any score. One Primary-day mask for every arm, built from observed power and existing detectors only and never from any arm's residual. An example is K1's G4 gate or the refit-offset neighbour-count detector (RO-NC) firing on 3 or more consecutive days. The unscreened score is primary and the screened score is reported beside it. Report D10 for 1 to 16 December 2025 as its own descriptive row.
    • Predictions that could fail.
      • Pooled over five months, FLOW-E minus G0 follow on bank M is at most −0.015, with the month-block 95% interval excluding zero.
      • FLOW-E minus C on bank M is at most −0.005, in at least 4 of the 5 months.
      • On bank X, FLOW-E is within 0.005 of G0. This is a known-answer check: with no mirror there is nothing to read.
      • FLOW-R follows at least 0.010 more than FLOW-E on bank M.
      • Six MW event-free CRPS: the upper 95% bound of FLOW-E minus G0 is below +0.044 on the screened rows, and the point estimate is at most +0.01.
      • The correction scores an area under the curve (AUC) of at least 0.75 on injected lag-days of bank M against K1's G4.

    • Stop rule. Stop the FLOW line if either of these holds:

      • the FLOW-E minus G0 follow interval on bank M includes zero;
      • FLOW-R is within 0.005 of FLOW-E. In that case write "the mirror helps, the wiring does not", and proposals 2 and 3 do not run.

      Report a CRPS failure on the screened rows as "not non-inferior". Proposal 3 may still run on the correction alone in that case. - GPU-hours. Experiment 651 cost about 0.125 GPU-h per arm-month-seed including three banks. That gives 37 new arm-month-seeds, about 4.6 GPU-h, plus about 0.5 GPU-h for C on banks S and X. Ceiling 6 GPU-h, one GPU process at a time. Time one FLOW-R cutoff first. Outputs about 8 GB, so delete the S and X bank parquet once they are scored. - Dependencies. Experiment 651's code (arm, grouped bank, scorer, gate) and the experiment 612 and 613 stored forecasts. The GPU is idle as of this writing. - Improves: the NRA forecast (follow) directly. The prevailing forecast gains only through the stored correction, which proposal 3 uses.

    2. FLOW-P: train the flow layer on the full 2019-onward history, with gap histories as inputs (only if proposal 1 passes)

    • Question. Does a flow layer trained from step 1 onward, on the full 2019-onward ERA5 and CAMS history with mirrored synthetic shifts, and reading several days of gap history, follow less than FLOW-E on banks M and S without costing anything on ordinary days?
    • Architecture changes.
      • The flow layer and its auxiliary loss start in step 1 and continue through steps 2 and 3.
      • The flow neural network reads each end's gap at the day lag and the week lag for the last 7 days (14 values per end, instead of 2), a MW-or-MVA flag per end, and the log capacity ratio.
      • Inject experiment 581's mirrored synthetic shifts into the pretraining rows at 5% of each Primary's time, as step 3 already does.
    • Training data. The full 2019-onward ERA5 and CAMS history in step 1, about 7 million rows per fold, against the fine-tuning window alone in experiment 651. Step 2 stays on ENS from 2024-04 so that the arms stay matched. The ENS backfill from 2021 is a separate question. Two caveats:
      • In pretraining the gap signal comes from a twin forecast driven by ERA5, which is less noisy than the ENS-driven twin used at forecast time. Scoring stays on ENS rows.
      • Unlabelled real shifts in the 2019 to 2024 targets teach every arm to follow. G0-P below controls for that.
    • Arms. FLOW-E (from proposal 1); FLOW-P (pretrained, two current gaps as inputs); FLOW-PH (pretrained, 7-day gap histories); and G0-P (the same pretraining bank of mirrored shifts, no flow layer). G0-P tests whether the synthetic bank in pretraining does the work by itself. Five months, seeds 0 and 1, the same screen as proposal 1.
    • Predictions that could fail.
      • FLOW-PH follows at most 0.030 on bank M and at most 0.032 on bank S (FLOW-E: 0.038 and 0.038).
      • FLOW-PH minus FLOW-E on bank M is at most −0.005, with the month-block interval excluding zero.
      • G0-P stays within 0.005 of G0 on bank M.
      • Six MW event-free CRPS of FLOW-PH minus G0 has an upper 95% bound below +0.044 on the screened rows.
    • Stop rule.
      • If FLOW-PH does not beat FLOW-E on bank M by 0.005, keep FLOW-E as it is and stop changing its inputs.
      • If G0-P matches FLOW-PH, the gain comes from the synthetic bank, not from the flow layer, and the flow layer is dropped.
    • GPU-hours. Steps 1 and 2 for 3 arms × 5 months × 2 seeds come to about 30 units at about 0.22 GPU-h with the grouped bank, which is about 6.5 GPU-h. Ceiling 8 GPU-h. Time one step-1 fit first and stop if it runs over 15 GPU-minutes.
    • Dependencies. Proposal 1 passes. A new grouped loader for the pretraining rows is the main code to write, and the leak test of experiment 651 is extended to it.
    • Improves: the NRA forecast.

    3. FLOW-H: the FLOW-E correction as evidence for the prevailing forecast (CPU first; only if proposal 1 keeps a working correction)

    • Question. Does the neighbour-mirror evidence that FLOW-E's correction carries improve the prevailing-conditions forecast of experiment 652, scored by the state of the shift at the origin?
    • Architecture changes.
      • Add the stored per-node correction for the last 7 days, and a "mirror present" summary (the correction's absolute value over the own gap), as inputs to 652's R-haz head.
      • Add the same two inputs to a B-haz variant whose survival curve is fitted separately for shifts with a mirror and shifts without one (no neural network).
      • Experiment 652's head is a few thousand weights and is fitted on the CPU.
    • Training data. Experiment 652's banks (experiment 650's extended generator, whose donor and receivers are conserved across real electrical neighbours, with no neighbour in 15% of shifts) and K1 on all 5 development months. FLOW-E inference runs on those banks, on the CPU as K1's did in experiment 650. The full history is not needed, because the head reads K1 and FLOW-E outputs only.
    • Arms. R-haz, R-haz+FLOW, B-haz, B-haz-mirror, R0-168, a placebo (R-haz+FLOW with the correction shuffled among the same Primary's origins), and K1 as context. 2 seeds.
    • Scoring. As experiment 652: event-free, far-from-end, middle, near-end, and just-ended origin strata, by lead day 1 to 9, scaled and unscaled, with the one veto (event-free cost against K1 at most 0.03).

    • Predictions that could fail.

      • With a shift in force (strata pooled), R-haz+FLOW minus R-haz scaled CRPS is at most −0.10, with the month-block interval excluding zero, on shifts that have a mirror.
      • On shifts with no neighbour, the same contrast is within ±0.05. The prediction holds only if that placebo-by-construction holds too.
      • On event-free origins, R-haz+FLOW is no worse than R-haz by more than 0.02.

      My prior that the first prediction holds is low, at about 25%. A shift's end reaches both ends' lags at the same delay of at least 24 h, so the mirror cannot reduce the near-end cost. Its only route to a gain is a better estimate of the depth, and fewer false departures. - Stop rule. If the in-force gain is above −0.05 or the placebo moves the score by as much, record that the electricity-network mirror does not help the prevailing forecast and close the FLOW line for the prevailing forecast. - GPU-hours. 0 planned. If FLOW-E inference on CPU is too slow, at most 1 GPU-h. - Dependencies. Proposal 1, with its stored correction for all five months. Experiment 652's code and banks. - Improves: the prevailing forecast only.

    0. The option of doing nothing more on FLOW-E

    The reasons for stopping here:

    • The follow gain is small. On bank M it is −0.006 against C and −0.021 against G0, and on bank S it is −0.008 against C. FLOW-E does nothing without a metered mirror (bank X equals G0).
    • Eight of the 14 scored Primaries report MVA, which cannot show the direction of a flow.
    • Real transfers can split across neighbours or go to Primaries off the roster.
    • Two verified real episodes is too few to judge on.
    • At 16 Primaries and 29 edges the electricity-network graph has little room, and fleet-scale adjacency (about 1,161 Primaries) has not been supplied.

    The reasons against stopping:

    • FLOW-E is the only arm of experiment 651 that held follow.
    • Its measured ordinary-day cost now looks like a scoring artefact of one probable unlabelled event.
    • FLOW-E is the "conservation along electrical edges" framing the roadmap already favours over learned message passing.
    • Proposal 1 costs at most 6 GPU-h and settles the question.

    My recommendation: run proposal 1 only. Run proposals 2 and 3 only if proposal 1 passes.

    Not a FLOW-E proposal, but changed by these analyses

    The "0.13-point excess over A1" that experiment 651 pointed at the class 2 recipe (E3 rows, D5 offset, 50% signal dropout) is about 45% the D10 episode, and the rest is mostly D16. Re-score that excess with proposal 1's screen on all five months before planning any recipe ablation. A recipe ablation that is planned against the unscreened number could chase a scoring artefact.

    Spec: MVA reverse flow, sign label and the hard-mask arm

    This is a copy of the sections of a Sonnet research answer of 2026-11-09 that the January restart needs, trimmed of conversation. "Evidence" means a merged page of this repository, and "inference" means the answering agent's own tabulation of the cleaned power table before 2026-07-01. The answer's survey of earlier experiments and its table of cases against the working assumption are left out; the experiment numbers cited below lead to them. The zero-fit experiment in the last section has not run. NGED's labels in the table below are the Reverse Power Flow? column of a private file. They are a hint, not truth.

    Short answer

    • The working assumption is mostly right but not always. The one real PV-driven case with a signed meter, D10, reverses only in April to August, 09:00 to 15:00 UTC (evidence for the direction, inference: 231 of 231 negative half-hours). At the eight MVA Primaries the best evidence is the same shape at D12. Counter-cases exist: one signed-MW series is negative at every hour of the day, and D8 has a gas engine (Gas A) behind it.
    • A hard high-PV mask is an established precision gate that costs recall. Experiment 417's A+H arm cut false reverse flow from 12.0% to 3.6% and cut recall from 0.77 to 0.37.
    • The eight cannot be calibrated where it matters. Experiment 508: nothing calibrates above an export of about 3 times the night import, and D12 is about 5.3 times.
    • Recommended first experiment: a zero-fit, CPU-only test of a per-Primary masked day labeller (last section).

    What NGED says, and where it conflicts with our data

    Source. The only record is data/NGED/substation_hierarchy_2026-10-15/Monitor Direction.csv (private; README says received 2026-10-15 from NGED), column Reverse Power Flow?. There is no confidence column and the Comments column is empty. I found no written record of NGED saying the label is only a hint; that statement is in the brief.

    Series Label
    Marsh Lane (D8) Yes
    Stickney (D12) Maybe
    Langrick (D22) Maybe
    Warth Lane (D2), Sleaford Road Boston (D3), Ingoldmells (D4), Chapel St Leonards (D19), Alford (D21) No

    Also, docs/background/network.md (this repository) says 10 sites are metered in apparent power (the eight plus two wind farms), and "one site, and possibly two more, have shown reverse flow on sunny days"; it gives no source. Experiment 79 records that NGED cannot supply real and reactive power at the eight.

    Conflicts with our data.

    • D8 "Yes". Experiment 370: L0 finds no reverse flow (share at most 0.006). Experiment 506: dips to 0.26 points on at least one sunny day. Experiment 508: 55 of 71 dip days flagged engine, not separable from solar or load transfer. NGED's "Yes" may refer to the engine, not to PV.
    • D12 "Maybe". Our data are far stronger than "Maybe" (3 independent fits, ratio 5.3, 656 fold map flags D12 midday in all six months). The one method that fails to see it is the 10:00 to 15:00 dip statistic (506), because the crossing is in the shoulders.
    • D22 "Maybe". 13 dip days, and the cloudy-day test does not support solar (508: 0 cloudy dips of 122 days, Fisher p 0.12). Fits find about 0% to 3.7% reverse. Unresolved.
    • The five "No". Consistent. D2, D3, D4 have lowest readings of 4.9 to 5.4 MVA (152); D19 0.92 and D21 1.6 MVA, both far above zero given their PV estimates.

    Known-answer routes and calibration limits

    Routes that exist.

    • Hide the sign at six MW Primaries (D5, D9, D10, D11, D14, D16) by taking the magnitude (reactive floor 0, 2, 4 points, or power factor 0.97), injecting a metered farm (Solar A to C) at levels 0.5 to 8 times the median sunny-midday reading, leaving the farm out in rotation (79, 358, 408, 412, 417, 420, 506, 508).
    • Real reverse flow with a signed meter: D10 (58 crossing days), D5 (5 days); 130 real half-hours in 417's count.
    • D12 itself has no truth. Closure against the bulk supply point cannot resolve the sign (152: coefficient 1.02, variance cut 0.7%; 506: only 58% to 71% of the children are metered).
    • Metered solar farm nearby (inference, not run as a test). docs/background/network.md shows D12's MVA peaks absent on 3 and 4 May when a nearby metered solar farm generated less. No experiment scored this as a label.
    • D8's engine: 44 days of the engine's own recorded output (experiment 194).

    Recommended first experiment

    First experiment (CPU only, zero fits): the per-Primary masked day labeller on known answers.

    • Mask. Per Primary, a half-hour is in the mask only if CAMS shape u is at least 0.1 (the sun-edge shoulders 508 found best; 0.3 would lose them) and the capacity gate holds: the Primary's 90th-percentile PV (474 R4) times u exceeds the dark-hour reading. The gate is per Primary because that series shows a global PV mask is wrong where non-PV export exists.
    • Label. Inside the mask, flag a day by 508's shoulder minimum (thresholds frozen on the six in ratio bins) and by experiment 417's W-shape score, and label as reverse flow the half-hours between the two minima on flagged days. Both rules exist already, so no new method is built.
    • Known-answer test. Injection of Solar A to C into D5, D9, D10, D11, D14, and D16 at levels 1, 2, and 3 with leave-one-farm-out, plus the no-injection placebo at the five clean Primaries, plus real D10. Score day-level area under the curve, half-hour recall at a false-reverse rate of at most 5%, and the placebo rate, by export-to-import bin as in 508.
    • Then apply to the eight and report a table of flagged days by Primary, sunny against cloudy, with no claim about truth. Check each Primary's mask coverage: share of its low (below 5 points) readings that the mask excludes.

    Prediction that could fail. At export-to-import 1.5 to 3, the masked labeller reaches half-hour recall of at least 0.6 at false-reverse of at most 5%, and its no-injection placebo rate is at most 1% at each of D5, D9, D11, D14, and D16, with thresholds frozen on the other Primaries. On the eight, flagged days exceed 10% of sunny April to August days at D12 only, are at most 5% at D8 and D22, and are 0 at D2, D3, D4, D19, and D21. A prediction written with these numbers can be wrong at either end, and would fail 417's bars if copied.

    Strongest reason it may fail. At low-demand Primaries (D5 and D10 mean sunny-midday reading 12 to 16 points against 38 to 53 elsewhere) the dip is a normal low-demand day: 427's labeller called 61.7% of D5's sunny middays reverse with nothing injected. At power factor 0.97 reactive power fills the dip (506: 34% false-flag). Above export of 3 times night import the method cannot be calibrated, and D12 is at 5.3 times. In short: the mask removes night and cloud false calls but not the low-demand sunny-day false calls, and no known answer exists at D12's export level.

    Not CPU, but higher value. Signed MW for a few weeks at Stickney from NGED (79's advice, repeated in 79's lessons) would settle D12 without a fit. I found no record that it was asked.

    Which change to the pooled mixture density network's output, or to its row weights, improves the tails of the Primary demand forecast beyond a global widening factor?

    Take-home message: widening the shared neural network's quantiles by 1.15 is the best of the fixes tested for its narrow outer bands, and none of the four tail changes beat it. At the six signed-MW Primaries (those whose power flows both ways, measured in signed megawatts) the widened pooled plain neural network (ctrl_w115) covers 0.891 of outcomes with its 95% quantile and 0.959 with its 99% quantile. Five mixture components, split-normal components, a widening fitted per lead band, and a neural network trained directly on quantiles each failed their registered test. No arm reaches the 0.92 to 0.98 band registered for the per-lead-band widening's q95 coverage. Five components was best on CRPS (continuous ranked probability score) among the four tail arms, and came closest to passing T1, but cut the q99 pinball loss by only 1.9% (0.354 against 0.361) against a 3% bar. The quantile-head neural network, widened, cut the q99 pinball loss by 15.8% (0.304) but raised the CRPS by 0.066 points of capacity (+0.052 to +0.078), so it fails T4. Two changes are worth testing next: the quantile-head neural network as a separate tail model, and a widening fitted on weeks closer to the test period. The verdict is not supported. Every number comes from five test months, so all are interim.

    The reference's median, 50% band, and 90% band against what happened, for D10 (a signed-MW Primary) and D4 (an MVA-only Primary): a day-ahead forecast and a full 14-day forecast from one run, made in winter (2026-01-12) and summer (2026-06-15).

    Predictions

    • T1 failed. With 5 components and the 1.15 widening, the q99 pinball loss at the signed-MW six is 0.354 against 0.361, 1.9% lower and short of the 3% bar. The CRPS is +0.008, inside the 0.02 limit.
    • T2 failed. The raw split-normal arm reaches q95 coverage 0.856, not 0.90, and its CRPS is +0.044 above the reference, not within 0.02.
    • T3 failed. The per-lead-band widening gives q95 coverage 0.856 (needed 0.92 to 0.98) and q99 coverage 0.943 (needed 0.97 or more). Its CRPS is +0.022, just over the 0.02 limit.
    • T4 failed. The raw quantile head's CRPS is +0.095 above the reference, outside the 0.03 limit (widened: +0.066). Its pinball loss on sunny low-demand half-hours is below the reference's at q01 (0.168 against 0.174) and above it at q05 (0.651 against 0.641). The widened head is above the reference at both (0.178 and 0.662).
    • H1 held. A half-life of 1 year is not better than the control by more than 0.02 at either group: +0.009 at the signed-MW six and +0.026 at the MVA-only eight.
    • H2 held. A half-life of 0.25 years is not better than the control: +0.073 and +0.025. At D15 and D18 (report-only) it scored -0.183 (-0.658 to +0.309), which spans zero.
    • C6 held. The start cut lowers the CRPS at D15 and D18 together for a0 by 0.324 (-0.638 to -0.028), and moves the signed-MW six by -0.017 and the MVA-only eight by +0.028, both under 0.03. The MVA-only eight interval (+0.010 to +0.047) lies above zero.
    • C7 held. With the cut, a0 minus the per-Primary plain neural network is -0.204 (-0.295 to -0.122) at the signed-MW six, below the -0.10 bar (experiment 403 found -0.185 without the cut).

    Decision

    The hypothesis is not supported, by the decision rule as written. All four of T1 to T4 failed, so no arm is carried forward. H1, H2, C6, and C7 do not count towards the decision. The smallest change worth carrying forward was a 3% lower q99 pinball loss at no CRPS loss; no arm met it.

    Components adopted or not adopted

    • Adopted, unchanged. The global 1.15 widening, the 0.5-year half-life, 3 Gaussian components, and the D15 and D18 start cut.
    • Not adopted. 5 components, split-normal components, the per-lead-band widening fitted on held-out weeks, the quantile head, and half-lives of 1 year and 0.25 years. The quantile head is worth testing as a tail model.
    • Attention over input groups (a0) is not adopted. a0 beats the per-Primary neural network (C7) but scores 3.334 at the signed-MW six against 3.302 for ctrl; the logs print no interval for that contrast.

    Limits

    • Interim. Five test months give wide intervals, most of all at D15 and D18, and no claim of benefit rests on any number here.
    • The widening fitted on held-out weeks barely changed the coverage. The logs do not print the fitted factors. The held-out weeks are rows the neural network never trains on, and they are older on average than the test months. The experiment did not test whether that age explains the result.
    • The avoidable-curtailment cost is not reported. The scorer logs hold no curtailment-cost figure.
    • Seed averaging. The widening was fitted and applied per seed and the three seeds were then averaged, so the averaged _bw forecast is not exactly calibrated.
    • Unlogged load-shift events. NGED says load-shift events may be missing from its logs, so the training rows and the scoring rows can hold events no label covers.
    • Cleaned data. Training rows drop every half-hour that the cleaned power table flags as an exact zero. The scorer dropped 1,179 flagged half-hours from the actuals of every arm, including the references from experiments 397 and 406, which were trained without this cleaning.
    • Departure: the widening is fitted on held-out weeks, not on a validation month, as the plan states. The load-shift event plots are not drawn.

    Problems caused by the move

    None.

    Plan as committed before any fit

    Question and design

    We change one thing at a time in the pooled plain network and compare each change with the same network widened by a global factor. Experiment 397 found that the networks cover too few outcomes with their 95% quantile at the six signed-MW Primaries, and that a widening of 1.15 only lifts the coverage to about 0.89. This experiment tests four ways to fix the tails and two row-weight half-lives.

    • The networks. The pooled plain network of experiment 397 (setting g09, summary scheme, an 8-wide Primary embedding) on its five screen folds (test months 2025-10, 2025-12, 2026-01, 2026-03, and 2026-06), with three seeds averaged in quantile space and the same refits and scoring rows.
    • ctrl. 3 Gaussian components, row-weight half-life 0.5 years. Its widened version ctrl_w115 is the reference.
    • k5. 5 Gaussian components.
    • skew. 3 split-normal components. A split-normal component has one standard deviation below its mode and another above it, so a component can be skewed. Each component's density and distribution function have closed forms, so the quantiles come from the same bisection as the Gaussian mixture. A Student-t family is not tested, because the Student-t distribution function has no closed form in the neural-network library used.
    • ctrl_bw: widening fitted per lead band. For each of five lead bands (day 1, days 2 to 3, days 4 to 7, days 8 to 10, days 11 to 14) and each of q01, q05, q95, and q99, the factor that makes the share of held-out rows below the widened quantile equal the nominal level. The held-out rows are the one week in every 12 that the network did not train on (the same rows that stop its training), so the widening is fitted inside each refit on rows the network has not seen. The factor is limited to between 0.5 and 4. Every arm also gets its _bw version, and the 1.15 _w115 version.
    • qhead. 11 quantiles output directly (a median and cumulative positive steps either side, so they cannot cross) and trained with the pinball loss averaged over the 11 levels.
    • hl365 and hl91. ctrl with row weights that halve every 1 year and every 0.25 years (the control halves every 0.5 years, as the XGBoost member does).
    • a0. Experiment 397's group-attention network with uniform weights, fitted with the start cut, to redo experiment 403's contrast.
    • The start cut. D15 and D18 lose every training row before 2025-03-01, and every row whose weekly lag reaches back before that date (maintainer's rule of 2026-10-30, from experiment 399). The scoring rows start in 2025-10, so no scoring actual or inference input is affected.
    • Input ports from XGBoost (the solar summary column and sunny flag with a 1-year half-life, and trailing temperature means) are not in this experiment and follow as a separate experiment.

    Components adopted from earlier results

    • Adopted. One pooled network with a Primary embedding (experiment 403: it scored lower than per-Primary networks); the ensemble mean and spread passed through the network once (experiment 397: within 0.05 points of 51 passes; experiment 394 found the mean carries most of the gain); the half-life of 0.5 years and monthly refits of the XGBoost member; the 1.15 widening of experiments 389 and 397; and the D15 and D18 start cut of experiment 399. Every arm of this experiment is therefore pooled, one-pass, widened where stated, and cut, and the arm names say what changed from that base.
    • Not adopted. Attention over input groups (experiment 397: the gate stayed within 0.01 of uniform), which survives only as the a0 arm that redoes experiment 403's contrast; and dropping the spread columns, which experiment 394 suggests is harmless but which no arm here tests.

    Standing practices followed, and the deviations

    This experiment follows the standing practices of the ml-experiment skill and common.md, and departs from two.

    • Followed. Monthly refits; the D15 and D18 start cut; every fitted network saved in the private data folder; stored forecasts reused as references and never refitted; a few-fold screen with every number labelled interim; tails and the avoidable-curtailment cost reported beside the CRPS; example forecast charts; a fresh Sonnet code review before the first fit.
    • Cleaned data. Training rows drop every half-hour that the cleaned power table flags as an exact zero (substation_zero), and every row whose lag reaches a flagged half-hour. The scorer drops flagged half-hours from the actuals of every arm, including the references from experiments 397 and 406. The number of rows dropped is printed for each fold. A forecast row whose inference input reaches a flagged lag half-hour is kept, because the flagged half-hours are rare (experiment 398 found 38 in the scored Primary half-hours), and the same rows enter every arm. The references from experiments 397 and 406 were trained without this cleaning.
    • Departure: the widening is fitted on held-out weeks, not on a validation month. The rule asks for a validation month inside the refit. A validation month would need a second fit per refit. The held-out weeks (one in every 12 across the 24-month window) are rows the network never trains on, so they play the same part at no extra fit, but they are older on average than the month before the refit.
    • Departure: the load-shift event plots are not drawn, because this experiment does not score performance during or after load-shift events.

    Fit counts, time, and memory

    • Fits. ctrl, k5, skew, qhead, hl365, and hl91: 6 arms by 5 folds by 3 seeds, so 90 pooled fits, and 15 more for a0. The widenings need no refit.
    • Time. Experiment 397 fitted a pooled plain network in about 50 s and A0 in about 130 s on the GPU, so the experiment is expected to take about 2.5 hours.
    • Memory. One GPU process with at most 8 GB of host memory and one feeder core. The per-Primary raw rows that experiment 397 wrote are read once per fold, a fold's tables are built once per half-life and shared by every arm and seed with that half-life, and the held-out rows' widening is computed once per fit.

    Checks the write-up reports

    • Absolute CRPS of every arm beside experiment 397's uncut networks, per group and per Primary.
    • Tails first. Coverage and pinball loss at q01, q05, q95, and q99, on all half-hours and on sunny low-demand half-hours, which are the sunny half-hours (experiment 381's flag) whose reference median is in the lowest quarter for the Primary. The rows are chosen from the forecast, never from the outcome.
    • Example forecasts. For D10 (signed-MW) and D4 (MVA-only), the reference's median, 50% band, and 90% band against what happened: one day-ahead forecast and one full 14-day forecast from a single run, in winter (made on 2026-01-12) and summer (made on 2026-06-15).
    • Unlogged load-shift events. NGED says load-shift events may be missing from its logs, so the training rows and the scoring rows can hold events no label covers.

    Caveats registered before the scores

    • Widening factors are fitted per seed and applied per seed, and the scorer then averages the widened quantiles over the three seeds, so the averaged _bw forecast is not exactly calibrated at the nominal level.
    • The widening is fitted on the unweighted held-out weeks across the whole 24-month window. For hl91, whose network concentrates on recent rows, the factors reflect older rows that the network fits less closely.
    • The sunny low-demand quarter is taken over all leads of the sunny half-hours, and the tail table then uses the days 1 to 10 rows of it.

    Counting towards the demand-v2 stop rule

    This experiment does not count. It tests changes to the tails of an existing model, not a new demand model, so no idea's count rises whatever the verdict.

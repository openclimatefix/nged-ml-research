    Which load-shift candidates does the fixed event table find at the 15 Primaries from 2026-07-01 to 2026-10-31?

    From 2026-07-01 to 2026-10-31 the event table of experiment 680, run unchanged on 15 Primary substations, finds 14 rows (13 events, because an edge transfer takes two rows). None of them explains the one-year backtest's flags at four Primaries. A Primary substation steps electricity down to a local distribution network. A load shift is a lasting change in a Primary's demand with no change in the weather, and an edge transfer is a load shift that moves between two neighbouring Primaries. The one-year backtest flagged time_series_id 13 (D18) as above its 95th-percentile forecast on 69% and 73% of half-hours, and 16 (D14) as below its 5th percentile on 75% and 78%. Flags at id 11 (D4) in September and id 18 (D16) in October were also reported.

    We ran the event table on the residual, which is the observed power minus the forecast of a convex demand model that uses no lagged power ("lag-free"), extended to the end of 2026-10-31. The table finds no edge transfer between ids 13 and 16 in July or August, and no event at id 11 in September.

    Every row is a candidate, never a label. NGED's event labels are an incomplete dump, so a clean half-hour may hold an event that no label covers, and an absence here is not proof that nothing happened. The final-test window 2026-07-01 to 2026-10-31 was read for this event table only, on the maintainer's authorisation. No detector was tuned on it, no method was chosen with it, and no forecasting model was scored on it. Noise unit has the meaning given in Method.

    The 14 rows starting in the window are 12 solo shifts (5 in July, 3 in September, 4 in October) and the two rows of the one edge transfer (the per-Primary and per-month counts are in the private SUMMARY.md).

    Reproduction gate

    • Refit cut. The 2026-06-01 cut of the lag-free forecast refitted here matches experiment 327's stored forecast with a maximum difference of 0 MW at all 15 series.
    • Window blanked. With the residual after 2026-06-30 set to missing, the pipeline reproduces experiment 680's 249 stored real-record events exactly (key, end, depth, and class).
    • Window included: 62 of 249 earlier events change. Of the 249 earlier events, 187 keep their Primary, start, class, and partners. The other 62 are replaced by 51 new ones, mostly small solo shifts spread over every Primary. The monthly refits and the median removal are ruled out as the cause. The slow level is refitted on the whole panel, so 4 more months of data move the baseline and the end-of-record handling; no run separated those two, so that cause is an inference by elimination. Among the 56 earlier events of 2 noise units or more that are neither ramps nor routine steps, 47 keep their key and move by a median of 0.05 noise units (largest 1.1), and none of the 47 has a changed end (22 of the 187 keyed matches in the full set do). The other 9 are dropped and 21 are added, mostly near the 2-unit threshold. Six of the added events are 4 noise units or more. Treat events from before July as possibly changed by the extension.

    Checks

    • Weather at forecast time. The lag-free forecast uses ERA5 2 m temperature and CAMS irradiance, both estimates of past weather. The table is a hindsight product and cannot be made live.
    • Last half-hour. The forecast for 2026-10-31 23:30 is missing because ERA5 ends at 23:00 on that day. Hours after 2026-06-30 are ERA5T in ARCO-ERA5, whose values are preliminary and may be revised.
    • Window power. Each Primary has 5,784 to 5,904 of 5,904 half-hours with a cleaned reading in the window. Half-hours flagged by the substation_zero cleaning rule are missing.
    • Code review. One fresh Sonnet code review ran before the run. It found a blocking indexing error, a dependence of the noise units on the series length, and a wrong dark flag on half-hour rows (a flag marking half-hours without daylight); all three were fixed. Every refit cut fitted.

    Method

    • Unchanged from experiment 680. E1-J-sym is experiment 653's event table plus two searches, with no parameter changed.
    • Input. The residual is the cleaned power minus the forecast of the lag-free convex demand model of experiment 130, as stored by experiment 327 (arm C, trained on every half-hour that has a reading, refitted on the 1st of each month on a 24-month window). Here the forecast is refitted for the cuts 2026-07-01, 08-01, 09-01, and 10-01, using experiment 327's own code path on a feature cache extended to 2026-10-31. The median residual across the 15 Primaries is removed before segmentation, with each Primary's scale fixed on the data before 2026-07-01. D18 loses every row before 2025-03-01.
    • Noise unit and slow level. One noise unit is the robust spread of the difference between successive 6-hour block means of the residual before 2026-07-01, frozen. The slow level is a seasonal level per Primary fitted with a Huber (1964) loss, as in experiment 653, and refitted on the whole extended panel.
    • Segmentation. Each Primary's residual, less the slow level, is cut into 6-hour block means. An exact dynamic programme decodes 25 states per block (no shift, and 12 amplitudes log-spaced from 1 to 20 noise units in each sign) with a Student-t emission, using the Viterbi (1967) algorithm, then refines each start and end to the half-hour. Events are classed as ramps, routine steps, permanent or open shifts, and paired across neighbouring Primaries (the graph of experiment 383) as in experiment 653.
    • Recipient search (E1-J, experiment 670). From each unpaired fall of 4 noise units or more, look at every connected Primary for a rise of 2 noise units or more starting near the fall, and accept up to three recipients whose combined rise conserves the fall under experiment 653's loose conservation cost.
    • Donor search (E1-J-sym, experiment 680). From each rise of 4 noise units or more still unpaired, look at every connected Primary for a fall of 2 noise units or more that starts up to 36 half-hours before the rise (widened by 6 half-hours on each side for start error), with a recipient share of 0.3 to 2.0 and a conservation cost of at most 2.5.
    • Output table. One row per event of 2 noise units or more that is not a ramp or routine step: the Primaries involved, class (edge transfer or solo shift), onset and end (end-of-half-hour stamps in UTC, or open), depth in megawatts and noise units, confidence, and whether the event starts before the window or ends after it. An edge transfer appears as two rows.
    • Compute. CPU only, 2 cores. The ERA5 extension is 2 m temperature only for August to October 2026 (1.6 MB), read from the ARCO-ERA5 store (Google's public copy of ERA5) with the stage-1 box script.

    Files

    • ml-experiments/00685-.../e00685_fetch_era5.py: runs the stage-1 ERA5 box fetch with its span set to 2026-08-01 to 2026-10-31.
    • e00685_inputs.py: extends experiment 103's row table, the ERA5 temperature, and the CAMS irradiance to 2026-10-31.
    • e00685_refit.py: refits experiment 327's arm C at the cuts 2026-06-01 to 2026-10-01 (the first is the gate) and overrides, in that process only, the three constants in the feature-cache and fitting code that exclude the window.
    • e00685_run.py: extends the power panel, removes the common median, runs E1-J-sym, checks the earlier events against experiment 680, and writes the tables.
    • Private tables, not in git: data/ml-experiments/00685-event-table-on-the-final-test-window-all-primaries/event_table/ (candidate_events.parquet and .csv, SUMMARY.md, reproduction_gate.json, and the lists of events only in the stored or only in the new table), and event_table_truncated_check/ for the window-blanked run. Inputs and forecasts: inputs/, e327_extended/, era5_box_extension/ in the same folder.

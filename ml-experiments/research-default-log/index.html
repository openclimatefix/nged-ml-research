    Log of research default versions

    This log lists every version of each family's research default, newest first. The rule page defines the families and the eight items in each row, and each family's evaluation protocol defines the adoption conditions. The pull request of the adopting experiment adds the row, and a row is never edited afterwards except to change its status.

    The adoption of Wind farms research default 2 overrules the rule. Experiment 347 passes the gain condition over every lead and the replicate condition, and fails the upper-tail condition and the gain condition over true days 1 to 10. A neural-network head given only the physics plant's median and the farm reproduces 83% of the gain over every lead. The maintainer adopted the arm anyway.

    The scored lead range is 0.5 hours to 15 days, and NGED's range is 1 to 10 days. The gain over true days 1 to 10 has an interval that includes zero.

    Experiment 348 tested four tail fixes inside the head and found none that calibrates the top quantile. Its blend of the physics plant's residual band with Wind farms research default 1's XGBoost quantiles gains less (0.147 points over true days 1 to 10) but leaves the 0.99 pinball loss unchanged. The blend is a candidate for a later version and does not change this adoption.

    Does the axial-attention demand network still forecast better than the Candidate when every history token reads only weather that a live service holds at the forecast origin?

    Weather history that can be built live gave the demand forecast about the same skill as laboratory weather history, and a small gain over the Candidate at the six signed-MW Primaries. The experiment asks whether experiment 713's neural network for substation demand, the axial-attention network ("the MSA"), keeps its lead when its inputs hold only data that a live service has at the forecast origin, which is the time at which the forecast is made. The network reads 14 days of history rows, and each history row holds the weather and power of one day. Experiment 713's selected arm M* fills the weather in those rows with analysis weather, which is weather reconstructed after the event from ERA5 (the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts' reanalysis) and CAMS (the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service's satellite irradiance estimate). A live service does not yet hold that analysis weather for the newest rows. We refitted the network as arm L, whose history rows hold only weather that a live service holds at the forecast origin. The Candidate C is the stored model of experiment 667. L1 is L minus C, and L2 is L minus M*, both in CRPS (continuous ranked probability score) points of capacity, where lower is better. Each contrast uses 5 seeds and 8 monthly folds. Each interval is a 98.75% month-block bootstrap interval, which resamples whole months of forecasts 100,000 times and reports the middle 98.75% of the resampled differences.

    The two groups of Primary substations are read separately. "The six signed-MW Primaries" are the 6 Primary substations whose telemetry is signed megawatts, and "the eight MVA-only Primaries" are the 8 whose telemetry is unsigned apparent power in megavolt-amperes (MVA). At the six signed-MW Primaries, L gave a small gain over C and no detectable difference from M*. At the eight MVA-only Primaries, L showed no detectable difference from C or from M*.

    The pre-registered rule gives the verdict small gain, read from L1 alone.

    Contrast (five seeds, 8 folds) Six signed-MW Primaries Eight MVA-only Primaries
    L1: L minus C -0.1522 [-0.2694, -0.0241], 5 of 5 seeds negative: small gain +0.0287 [-0.0882, +0.1130]: no detectable difference
    L2: L minus M* -0.0050 [-0.0102, +0.0008], 3 of 5 seeds negative: no detectable difference +0.0066 [-0.0095, +0.0350]: no detectable difference

    The reading is "small gain" and not "gain", because the t guard fails. The t interval over the 8 monthly differences is -0.3316 to +0.0397 at the six signed-MW Primaries, so it includes zero. The contrast is negative in 7 of 8 months (2026-06 is +0.1307). The guards for supported also fail: the 95% upper bound of the q01 pinball loss of L minus C at the six signed-MW Primaries is +0.0505, above the limit of 0.05, and at the eight MVA-only Primaries leaving out one Primary flips the sign of L1. All four point estimates fell inside the ranges that the plan predicted before any fit, and the predicted verdict was small gain or inconclusive.

    Two limits apply to every number on this page. Experiment 713's known-answer gate failed two of its checks and the maintainer waived it, so no arm here makes a statement about following level shifts or recognising an era. The target row of every arm (C, M*, and L) still reads the CAMS mean and clear-sky ratio of UTC day D - 1, which a live service may not hold at 09:00 UTC on day D. L1 is therefore like for like in the history weather, and neither score is a live score.

    The maintainer parked the experiment after phase 6, because the question was already answered and the remaining arms were not worth the compute and quota. Phases 0 to 3 and phase 6 ran. Phase 5 (the budget probe) did not run, because the probe flag did not fire: L2 was below the two-seed noise floor in both groups, and 0 of 16 fits of L were flagged. Arm E (phase 7) did not run. Phase 4, the T3 read-out, started and was stopped (see "What was not run"). No attention map and no forecast time-series plot was produced, because the experiment was parked, and the page has no figure.

    What was run

    • Phase 0 (wiring checks and reproduction): all checks KL1 to KL10 passed under checks_hash 9b4b6cddf6122323. Arm Z, the refit with no weather change, was fitted on fold 2025-11, seed 0. Its best validation loss matched the stored M* fit to 0.0 nats in stage 2 and in stage 3, and its CRPS equalled the stored M* CRPS (2.8113 at the six signed-MW Primaries, 3.4218 at the eight MVA-only Primaries). The reproduction check passed.
    • Phases 1 and 2: L on 8 folds, seeds 0 and 1, and S predictions on the same folds. S is the stored stage 3 state of M* predicting on the live rule with no retraining.
    • Phase 1 quick look (interim: 4 folds, 2 seeds, 95% intervals): L minus C was -0.0944 [-0.1976, +0.0229] at the six signed-MW Primaries and -0.0651 [-0.1476, +0.0360] at the eight MVA-only Primaries. L minus M* was -0.0066 [-0.0133, +0.0028] and +0.0082 [-0.0083, +0.0312]. S minus C was -0.0914 [-0.1864, +0.0356] and -0.0741 [-0.1495, +0.0327], and S minus M* was -0.0036 [-0.0249, +0.0123] and -0.0008 [-0.0056, +0.0061]. The audit rule for a large L minus M* did not fire.
    • Phase 3 (arm Z on the quick-look folds, then the counted two-seed reading): Z minus M* was exactly 0.0 on every fold and seed in both groups, so audit trigger 1 did not fire. The two-seed reading (8 folds, 98.75%) gave L1 -0.1612 [-0.3140, -0.0251] at the six signed-MW Primaries (2 of 2 seeds negative) and +0.0283 [-0.0801, +0.1337] at the eight MVA-only Primaries. L2 was -0.0108 [-0.0274, +0.0008] and +0.0008 [-0.0124, +0.0185]. The verdict word was the same as the five-seed reading, and the futility rule did not fire, because L1 was below zero at the six signed-MW Primaries.
    • Phase 6: L on 8 folds, seeds 2 to 4, giving the five-seed reading in the table above.

    What was not run

    • Phase 5 (budget probe), arm E, and phase 7: not run, for the reasons above. The page therefore has no reading of L minus E, and no statement about dropping the ERA5T or half-hourly CAMS feeds.
    • T3 (the Candidate, M*, and L predicting with the target row's CAMS day summary moved from D - 1 to D - 3): phase 4 started and was stopped when the experiment was parked. These files exist.
      • T3-M: 4 prediction files (folds 2025-11 and 2025-12, seeds 0 and 1) under work/fits/T3-M/, and 4 logs.
      • T3-C: one one-off run for fold 2025-11, seed 0, under t3_candidate/2025-11/, with its patched trailing-power table, its prediction file, and log_s0.json.
      • T3-L: no file.

    No T3 reading is made on this page and none is quoted, because a T3 contrast needs all three arms on the same folds and seeds. The share of L1 that rests on the target row's CAMS day summary is unknown. - Plots of attention maps and of forecast time series: not produced, because the experiment was parked. - The median minus the mean per site: not computed, because the stored scoring files hold no per-site mean difference.

    Compute

    The experiment spent 3.54 GPU-process-hours, as recorded in work/gpu_hours.json, against a reporting ceiling of 12.0 and projections of 6.04 (phase 0) and 5.34 (after phase 1). The projection of 5.34 is in work/projection.json, and the 6.04 comes from the manager's brief and is not in a stored file. The spend is lower than the projections because phases 5 and 7 did not run and phase 4 stopped early.

    The two tallies differ because e00725_phases.py hours skips the wiring-check jobs. The raw sum of work/gpu_hours.json is 12,728 s, or 3.5356 h: 2.0246 h of fits, 1.1589 h of predictions, and 0.3521 h of 16 wiring-check jobs (one train and one predict job for each of the 8 folds), which ran with --device cuda. The hours command sums every key except those that contain " CHECK ", which gives 3.1835 h. Phase 6 (1.44 h) and the smoke fit (149.6 s) are in both tallies. The exact figure is 3.54 h, the file's sum, because the check jobs held a GPU. Both tallies miss work run outside the runner or after its last write: the T3-M prediction of fold 2025-12, seed 1 (52.8 s in work/logs/T3-M/2025-12_s1_predict.json, whose output exists), the one-off T3-C prediction (49.6 s in t3_candidate/2025-11/log_s0.json; its fit time is not in that file), and the read-only comparison runs of Fix A. We did not time the comparison runs, and estimate them at well under 0.2 h.

    Fix A: the T3 known-answer check

    The T3 known-answer check failed in 5 of 8 folds because of a fault in what the check expected. A read-only Opus audit found that the failing pick in each fold was the last training origin, 12:00 UTC on the day before the fold's cutoff. The cutoff cuts that origin's target row in half. The parent source holds zero weather at the unfilled half-hours, and LiveSource wrote the moved CAMS summary into them. No arm, stored fit, or stored prediction was wrong, and T3-L, T3-M, and T3-C predict only on fully filled rows.

    Fix A writes the moved summary only where the ensemble row exists, and the stored states are read with the old code hash.

    Two read-only comparisons of the code before and after Fix A found no difference on a filled half-hour. On the predict phase of folds 2025-11, 2026-04, 2026-01, and 2026-02, every sample is bit-identical, and the maximum absolute difference over every tensor of the 2,832 samples of folds 2026-01 and 2026-02 is 0.0. On the train phase of folds 2026-04, 2026-01, and 2025-12, 40 of 42 samples are bit-identical. The other 2 are the fold's last origin, and their 78 differing half-hours are all unfilled. A second audit round confirmed the diff. The stored L, Z, and E states are read with --accept-code-change dad2fdbd70a1b0fb, because the changed branch never runs in a fit. The wiring checks ran again under the new checks_hash and passed.

    Checks

    • Audit triggers: trigger 1 (Z minus M* above the two-seed noise floor) did not fire. The quick-look audit rule (a large L minus M*) did not fire. The probe flag did not fire (L2 above the floor in neither group, 0 of 16 fits flagged). The T3 known-answer failure was an implementation-check fault, audited by a read-only Opus audit, fixed with Fix A, and re-checked.
    • Reproduction: Z equals M* to 0.0 on best validation loss and on CRPS, and the check passed. The across-seed standard deviation of fold 2025-11's CRPS is 0.0759 at the six signed-MW Primaries and 0.0627 at the eight MVA-only Primaries.
    • Look-ahead statement (KL8): L and the inputs of E show no negative margin, and Z shows negative margins of up to 111 h by design. The read of E is the wiring-check read of the E inputs, because E was never fitted.
    • Experiment 713's work folder: its hash was unchanged before and after every phase.
    • Guards for supported: not met. The q01 pinball guard fails at the six signed-MW Primaries, and the leave-one-Primary-out guard fails at the eight MVA-only Primaries. The t guard fails the "gain" reading at the six signed-MW Primaries. None of the three changes the verdict word, because the verdict is small gain.
    • Not produced or not run: the attention-map plots, the forecast time-series plots, T3, and L minus E. "What was not run" says why.
    • Host memory: the largest peak in work/memory_peak/ is 7,993,483,264 B (7.99 GB), from the prediction of Z for fold 2026-06, seed 1 (e00725_predict.py_Z_2026-06_1.txt). The peak of 6,639,824,896 B is the T3-M prediction for fold 2025-12, seed 1, and is not the largest. Fits, predictions, and per-fold checks ran under run.sh's 9 GB scope (MemoryMax=9G).
    • Median minus mean per site: not computed.

    Method

    • Architecture and training regime: experiment 713's MsaNet, stages 2 and 3, warm-started from the stored stage 1 state of M* of the same fold and seed, with frozen encoders and no new parameter. Each arm differs from the others in the history weather alone.
    • History weather of arm L: each history token takes the ENS-mean weather of the stored run that a live service holds at the 09:00 UTC native origin (a run issued at least 9 h earlier) at ages 2 to 6, and keeps analyses only where the lags allow them. CAMS analyses are used up to UTC day D - 3 (a lag of 36 h after the end of the day), and ERA5 analyses from age 7 (a lag of 120 h). The rule changes 3,763 of the 14,112 weather values of a history matrix (27%). Training cells with no stored ENS row fall back to the analysis, and scored cells with no row read zero.
    • Measurement: CRPS in points of capacity on leads from 24 h up to but not including 240 h after the 12:00 UTC origin, on experiment 713's rows and 8 monthly folds, month-block bootstrap at 98.75% with 100,000 resamples, no valid time on or after 2026-07-01 scored. Seed s of one arm is paired with seed s of the other. The noise floors and δ are experiment 713's pre-registered values.
    • Differences for the other arms: C and M* are stored forecasts, never refitted. S is the stored M* state predicting on the live rule with no retraining. Z is M*'s inputs refitted through this experiment's code. E (ENS at every age) and T3 were planned and not run.
    • Standing practices followed: monthly refit, cleaned data, fitted states saved outside git, stored forecasts reused as controls, and a screen on 4 folds first. Plots and the budget probe were not produced, as above.

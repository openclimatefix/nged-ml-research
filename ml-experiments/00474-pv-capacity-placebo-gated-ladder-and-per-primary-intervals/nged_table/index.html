    Season-year July 2025 to June 2026, in megawatts. Capacities come from the pooled solar-shape model driven by the mean of ERA5 and CAMS irradiance, with two cloud terms; D12 uses the fit through the meter.

    Confidence column. Coverage is the share of injected metered-farm capacities that the 80% interval contained in tests, first without and then with a cloud-following load. Placebo is the 95th percentile of the share of a fake farm (a day-of-year profile with another day's anomaly) that the fit found. The placebo test fails when it exceeds 0.20, so it failed on every row. Status terms: MVA-only means the reading is apparent power with no sign; the other Primaries have signed readings. D12 shows its season-year 2024 value (July 2024 to June 2025), the latest the fit through the meter was run for.

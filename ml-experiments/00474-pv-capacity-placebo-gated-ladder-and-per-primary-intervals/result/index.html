    PV capacity: a placebo-gated ladder and a per-Primary table with intervals

    How to read the NGED table. The table is in nged_table.md, beside this page. Each row is one Primary, meaning one primary substation. The capacity columns give the 10th, 50th, and 90th percentile, across bootstrap resamples of the fit, of the estimated solar capacity behind that substation's meter, in megawatts. AC capacity is what the inverters can export. DC capacity is the panels' rating, assumed to be 1.1 to 1.5 times the AC capacity. A narrow range does not mean a certain estimate. The Confidence column says how well the method found a metered solar farm that we added to a Primary's reading as a test, and the Status column names Primaries that need extra care. Every row's placebo test failed (the next paragraph explains the test).

    Take-home message (generalised additive model side, interim): no candidate solar shape passes the placebo test, and a cloud-load term is what makes the capacity ranges honest. The test of a candidate method, called a rung, injects a metered solar farm's output into a Primary's reading and asks the convex fit of experiment 129 to find it again. Recovery is the share of the injected output the fit finds (1 is all of it). Without the cloud terms, each rung recovers 0.75 to 0.94. The placebo test injects a fake farm that keeps the season and time of day but not the real daily weather: the farm's day-of-year profile plus the anomaly of another day of the same month. A rung passes only if the 95th percentile of the placebo's recovery stays under 0.20. It reaches 0.25 to 0.40 (the maximum over Primaries), because the profile alone recovers 0.13 to 0.26 (0.39 to 0.41 once the cloud terms are added). A cloud-following load (extra demand on cloudy days) lowers the 80% interval's coverage of the injected capacity to 0% to 24% at every rung without the cloud terms. Two smooth cloud terms in the fit (day-mean and half-hour cloud response) restore coverage to 73% to 93%, and raise the placebo to 0.49 to 0.51. The table therefore uses the rung with the best coverage in both cases: the pooled plant table driven by the mean of ERA5 and CAMS irradiance, with the cloud terms (recovery 0.87).

    Figures

    Ladder results

    Raw recovery over 6 Primaries (D5, D9, D10, D11, D14, D16) and 5 farms, with injections of 2 and 4 MW. Placebo, profile-only, and cloud-load tests use 2 MW. Placebo figures are the 95th percentile over 20 shuffles, taken as the maximum over Primaries. Coverage figures are the 80% interval widened by the spread of recoveries, except where C0 also gives the bootstrap alone. Numbers are in the private file work/rung_summary.parquet; the main rows follow. CAMS is the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service's satellite irradiance, and ERA5 is the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts' reanalysis (an estimate of past weather). A rung is one candidate solar shape: C rungs come from experiments 129 and 269 (C0 is the mean learned plant response of the other farms read at the Primary's irradiance, and C1 and C1b are physics shapes at two panel tilts), and R rungs were added in this experiment.

    • C0, pooled plant table (CAMS sky): recovery 0.874, placebo 95th percentile 0.36 (maximum over Primaries), coverage 27% (bootstrap alone) and 80% (bootstrap plus recovery spread); with a cloud- following load 0% and 7%.
    • C1, physics shape, tilt 20 degrees: 0.823, placebo 0.28, coverage 75%; cloud load 0%.
    • C1b, physics shape, tilt 35 degrees: 0.752, placebo 0.25, coverage 75%; cloud load 0%.
    • R1, nearest farm's recorded output as the shape (instead of CAMS): 0.876, placebo 0.35, coverage 79%; cloud load 24%.
    • R2, nearest farm's learned plant response: 0.881, placebo 0.36, coverage 86%.
    • R3, pooled table plus nearest farm's output (as well as CAMS): 0.938, placebo 0.37, coverage 79%.
    • R4, pooled table driven by the mean of ERA5 and CAMS irradiance: 0.940, placebo 0.40, coverage 85%; cloud load 3%.
    • R5, pooled table driven by ERA5 alone: 0.814, placebo 0.40, coverage 77%.
    • With the cloud terms: C0 0.882 (placebo 0.49, coverage 77%, cloud load 73%); C1 1.030 (0.51, 80%, 80%); R4 0.868 (0.51, 93%, 93%).

    Table for NGED (nged_table.md)

    The table is in nged_table.md (latest season-year) and in the private file work/nged_table.parquet (every season-year). The table uses rung R4 with the cloud terms. DC capacity is AC capacity times an assumed ratio between 1.1 and 1.5 (centre 1.25), because experiment 73 found that the ratio cannot be fitted.

    Where the neural estimate is reported (experiment 477)

    The neural estimate and the pros-and-cons scores are on the page of experiment 477, which also holds the side-by-side plot of neural and GAM capacities. Experiment 477 reads this experiment's files: the table nged_table.md (latest season-year), and the private files work/nged_table.parquet (every season-year, with the columns ac_p10, ac_p50, ac_p90, dc_p10, dc_p50, dc_p90, pinned_mw, status) and work/rung_summary.parquet, under data/ml-experiments/00474-pv-capacity-placebo-gated-ladder-and-per-primary-intervals/.

    Caveats

    • No rung passes the placebo gate, so every capacity carries a "gate failed" note.
    • D12 uses the physical fleet shape and the fit through the meter; D15 is a generator case with no estimate; D18 has data only from 2025-03-01.
    • MVA-only Primaries are fitted to apparent power with reactive power assumed zero, which experiment 370 found fails at a power factor of 0.97.
    • The cloud-load test uses one form of load (experiment 129's), and the cloud terms match that form exactly, so the restored coverage is an optimistic figure.
    • NGED's event labels are incomplete, so the readings may hold unlabelled events.
    • Deviations from the plan: 20 placebo shuffles ran at every rung (the plan's metric line said 5 at some rungs); the injection test ran at 6 Primaries and the prediction text counts 5; C3 (a rise allowed, a fall resisted) was dropped because the fit already boxes every capacity increment to be non-negative, so the capacity path cannot fall; C2 was applied after the fit as an assumed ratio.
    • The D12 bootstrap ran about 30 resamples (each fit takes about 40 seconds).

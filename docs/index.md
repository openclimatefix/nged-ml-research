# NGED ML research

**This site holds the machine-learning experiments and studies for the NGED substation forecast.**
The production service, its architecture, and its roadmap are documented in the
[nged-substation-forecast docs](https://openclimatefix.github.io/nged-substation-forecast/).

**The two kinds of research page live in two sections.** The [Studies](studies/index.md) section
holds open-ended studies, each with a page of its own. The [ML
experiments](ml-experiments/README.md) section holds the small, focused experiments that test one
change to the research default, together with the research defaults themselves.
